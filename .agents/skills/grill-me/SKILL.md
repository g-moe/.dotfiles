# Workflow

1. Investigate the task or feature if research is required. Research can come from repository branches, or online reputable sources (third-party docs, third-party github repos, third-party clis, etc). Use best judgement to size how much investigation is needed.

2. Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one.
   2a. For each question, give options and your recommended answer with the reason. Do not use any question tools...only use text.
   2b. Ask questions one at a time. Do not move on to the next question until I choose an option.
