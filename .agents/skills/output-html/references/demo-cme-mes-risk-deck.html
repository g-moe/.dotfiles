05 / Before order entry

Risk math is a gate, not a forecast.

  • Confirm the exact contract and expiration month.
  • Place the stop at the trade's invalidation point, not at a convenient dollar amount.
  • Convert the stop distance to dollars and add an explicit cost buffer.
  • Floor the size calculation; never round up.
  • Check total exposure, session limits, scheduled events, and liquidity.
  • Know how the position and protective order will be exited.
