02 / Govern the session

Risk math is a gate, not a forecast.

After a loss: pause, reduce, or stop. Set the boundary before the heat of the moment.

  • Confirm the exact contract and expiration month.
  • Place the stop at the invalidation point.
  • Convert the stop to dollars and add a buffer.
  • Floor the size calculation. Never round up.
  • Check exposure, session limits, events, and liquidity.
